Demo privacy

Updated 8 October 2026. This notice applies to the SeekApp Scope live demo, operated by Truong Can Em in Vietnam. Contact contact@seekapp.net.

When you analyze a brief

After you consent and select Analyze with Claude, your brief and chosen output language are sent through our Cloudflare Worker to Anthropic’s Claude API. Cloudflare and Anthropic process the request under their respective terms and policies. Please use fictional or non-sensitive briefs: do not submit credentials, personal data or confidential customer information.

Storage and retention

The demo does not intentionally save brief text or generated proposals in our database, application logs, cookies or browser storage. Briefs and edits remain in the current browser page and disappear when it is closed or reloaded. Downloaded or copied drafts remain wherever you save them. Provider-side handling and retention are governed by Anthropic’s privacy information and Cloudflare’s privacy policy; this demo makes no zero-retention promise for those providers.

Verification and usage limits

Cloudflare Turnstile processes verification signals. The Worker sends your source IP to Turnstile for verification. Our rate limiter stores a salted hash of your source IP, counters and short-lived request reservations in a Cloudflare Durable Object. Rate-limit windows last ten minutes; the stored budget resets on the next accepted request on a new UTC day. The demo disables application observability by default, but Cloudflare can still process operational and security metadata.

Sharing and human review

We do not automatically email a proposal or send it to your client. Editing, copying and exporting occur in your browser. Do not share outputs without checking them.
